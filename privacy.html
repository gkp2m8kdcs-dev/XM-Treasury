Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

XM Treasury ("the App", "we", "us") is built around a single principle: your financial journal belongs to you, and it stays on your device. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data the App does and does not collect, how any optional permissions are used, and the rights you have over the information stored locally on your device.

By installing or using XM Treasury, you agree to the terms set out below.

Tracking Declaration

XM Treasury does not track you. "Tracking" in this policy means any activity by which user or device data is collected, linked to an identifier (such as an Apple advertising identifier or any persistent user ID), or shared with a third party for the purpose of advertising, attribution, cross-app behavior analysis, or any form of remote profiling.

Specifically, XM Treasury:

  • Contains no third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel, no Adjust, no AppsFlyer, no Branch, etc.).
  • Contains no advertising networks or mediation layers.
  • Does not access or transmit the Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers).
  • Does not perform fingerprinting, device-graph joining, or probabilistic identification.
  • Does not include any crash-reporting service that transmits stack traces or device identifiers (no Crashlytics, no Sentry, no Bugsnag).
  • Does not include A/B testing, feature flagging, or remote configuration services that transmit usage data.
  • Does not include social login, third-party sign-in, or OAuth flows.

This Tracking Declaration is aligned with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework and supports the App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

XM Treasury does not collect, transmit, store, sell, rent, lease, or share any of the following, because the App has no server, no account system, and no telemetry path of any kind:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, address, government ID).
  • Financial account credentials of any kind (no bank linking, no brokerage linking, no brokerage API keys).
  • Transaction history, holdings, budgets, goals, category definitions, receipts, or any journal content you create.
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, MAC addresses, IMEI, or serial numbers.
  • Location data, contacts, calendars, or health data.
  • Usage analytics, behavioral events, session length, click-throughs, or feature usage.
  • Crash reports, diagnostic data, performance metrics, or device telemetry.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data only on your device, inside the app's sandbox container, accessible solely to XM Treasury and the operating system:

Data TypePurposeShared?
Accounts, transactions, holdings, budgets, goals, categoriesCore journal functionalityNot shared
Receipt photos you attachAttach to a transaction as supporting evidenceNot shared

Local data is never sent off-device by the App. If you delete the App, its sandbox container is removed by iOS and all local data is permanently erased.

3. Optional Permissions

XM Treasury requests only the permissions strictly required for the features you choose to use. You can decline any of these permissions and the App will continue to function without the corresponding feature.

Camera

Purpose: Capture a photo of a paper receipt to attach to a transaction. Photos are stored only inside the App's sandbox.

Data flow: The captured image is written directly to the App's local document directory. It is not uploaded, not transmitted, and not processed by any server.

Photo Library

Purpose: Pick an existing image (for example, a receipt you already photographed) to attach to a transaction. Selected images are copied into the App's sandbox; the original is left in your library untouched.

Data flow: The chosen asset is imported into the App's local document directory. It is not uploaded, not transmitted, and not processed by any server.

If you previously granted Camera or Photo Library access and wish to revoke it, you can do so at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security on your device.

4. Children's Privacy

XM Treasury is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because the App collects no data at all, no children's data can be processed.

5. International Users

XM Treasury does not transfer, store, or process any personal data outside of your device. There are no cross-border data flows because there are no data flows of any kind.

6. Your Rights and Controls

Because all of your data lives on your device, you are always in full control of it. Specifically:

  • Access: Open XM Treasury to view every account, transaction, holding, budget, goal, and receipt.
  • Edit & delete: Every record can be modified or removed from inside the App.
  • Export: Use the in-app export feature to produce a backup file that you control.
  • Erase: Delete the App from your device to permanently remove all locally stored data.
  • Questions: Contact the developer at huelmelissa1841991@gmail.com.

7. Legal Basis for Processing

Because the App does not transmit or process personal data off-device, there is no off-device legal basis to disclose. Any on-device processing (displaying your journal, generating charts) is performed at your direction and under your control.

8. Changes to This Policy

If the App's data practices ever change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the change takes effect, the "Last updated" date above will be revised, and — if the change is material — you will be notified the next time you open the App and asked to acknowledge the updated policy.
