Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
All of your data is stored locally on your iPhone, inside XM Treasury's secure sandbox container. Nothing is uploaded to a server. If you uninstall the App, the data is permanently deleted with it.
Can I back up or transfer my data to a new device?
Yes. Use the in-app export feature to create a backup file, then transfer it to your new device using AirDrop, Files, iCloud Drive, or any other method you choose. Restore by re-installing XM Treasury on the new device and importing the file. Because the App does not transmit data automatically, this is the only XM Treasury is involved in.
How do live prices and net worth get updated?
They don't — by design. XM Treasury is a journal, not a market data feed. Every holding has a current price that you entered. Updating a single holding, or updating many at once from a list, recalculates that holding's market value, gain/loss, and the portfolio total.
What permissions does the App request, and why?
Only two optional permissions are ever requested: Camera, to capture receipt photos, and Photo Library, to attach existing images to transactions. Both are 100% optional. If you decline, the App remains fully functional except for that specific attachment feature.
Is XM Treasury an investment advisor?
No. XM Treasury is a personal journaling tool. It does not provide investment advice, does not recommend securities, does not execute trades, and does not act as a custodian. All numbers shown inside the App are computed from the entries you provide. See the Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.
I forgot my App settings — how do I reset?
Open XM Treasury → Settings → About → Reset Preferences. Note that this does not delete your data, only application preferences. To permanently delete all data, you must uninstall the App.
Does the App track me in any way?
No. XM Treasury contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting that transmits data, and no tracking of any kind. Please see the Privacy Policy and the Tracking Declaration below.

Tracking Declaration

XM Treasury does not track users. The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or financial data with the developer or any third party. The App contains no tracking SDKs, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no cross-app tracking. Support requests that you send by email are handled directly by the developer and are used only to respond to your question.

Status & Compatibility

The current release supports iOS 16.0 and later on iPhone. The App is fully offline-capable; an internet connection is never required to use it.

If you encounter a problem after a recent iOS update, please email support and we will work with you to find a fix.
